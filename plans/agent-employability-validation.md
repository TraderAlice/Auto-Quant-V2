## Outcome

Demonstrate that fresh coding coworkers, given only an installed AutoQuant
release, a caller-style assignment, and staged data, can independently choose
the right research route, preserve caller intent, operate the authorized
surface, interpret evidence correctly, stop at the right boundary, and return
an answer that addresses the caller's decision.

The result is a durable employability conclusion over materially different
tasks, not another list of supported SDK features.

## Context

AutoQuant has passed its architectural feasibility stage. Real-data Projects
already prove Factor, Portfolio, governed RL, fixed Allocation, Event Study,
reported-book risk, multi-interval, mixed-class, and negative-evidence
capabilities. Later releases also used isolated installed-wheel Grok workers
to expose and retry individual Agent-facing defects.

Those records answer two different questions:

1. can AutoQuant represent and execute this research method?
2. can an unfamiliar coworker receive an ordinary request and reliably deliver
   the right result without framework-developer coaching?

The first question has broad evidence. The second has several strong examples
but no common protocol, scorecard, coverage audit, or stopping rule. Until that
evidence is consolidated and extended, integrating OpenAlice risks freezing an
accidental current workflow instead of consuming a stable independent desk.

## Scope

### In scope

- Maintain a Markdown-first employability protocol and ledger over historical
  and fresh worker trials.
- Grade evidence by worker isolation and recoverability rather than by whether
  the market result was positive.
- Run fresh workers against materially different caller-style assignments,
  with no private repository knowledge or corrective coaching.
- Record route selection, clarification, editable-boundary behavior, CLI
  recovery, scientific interpretation, restraint, final-answer usefulness,
  runtime, and immutable evidence.
- Promote only repeated friction or one severe scientific/authority failure
  into a separate Core plan, then retry with a new worker.
- Derive an explicit gate for later OpenAlice consumption work.

### Out of scope

- Adding models, data types, or trading features merely to make the benchmark
  look broad.
- Building a benchmark service, rigid task schema, weighted leaderboard, or
  automatic natural-language router.
- Teaching a trial worker through source code, repository tests/plans, prior
  Project answers, web research, subagents, or framework-developer hints.
- Treating clarification questions, REVERT, negative evidence, or an honest
  unsupported boundary as failure.
- Live account access, Broker/Order authority, or OpenAlice-specific
  orchestration and transport.

## Findings and decisions

- 2026-07-30 — The existing Goal already states the governing experiment:
  fresh Grok workers perform research while a separate Agent improves the
  Workbench. This plan formalizes that Goal rather than replacing it.
- 2026-07-30 — Existing method coverage is broad, but historical
  framework-developer Runs do not automatically count as employability proof.
  Worker isolation and final-answer quality must be evidenced independently.
- 2026-07-30 — `0.8.28` is the first seed in the final cohort: one fresh
  installed-wheel worker completed a daily Factor negative result with zero
  CLI retry and a truthful terminal answer.
- 2026-07-30 — Numeric market outcomes are not pass criteria. A correct
  REVERT, baseline retention, explicit refusal, or finding of no stable rule
  may be a stronger coworker result than a positive backtest.
- 2026-07-30 — The proposed
  [[plans/optional-post-trial-research-agenda]] remains a separate small
  discoverability issue. It is recorded in the ledger but is not allowed to
  expand this validation plan into a feature bundle.
- 2026-07-30 — The durable outward handoff is the worker output required for
  audit. Private reasoning/chain-of-thought is neither necessary nor useful
  acceptance evidence.
- 2026-07-30 — Four historical isolated-worker cases qualify after
  independent replay: a multistage Factor gate, a two-Project fixed desk, a
  capped Allocation fidelity audit, and a manifest-root Event Study. They
  establish a strong baseline but do not replace the required post-plan cohort.
- 2026-07-30 — Evidence quality and worker smoothness are separate. The
  `0.8.19` multistage trial has Grade A preserved evidence and a correct answer,
  but its two Report-reference retries make it a high-friction pass rather
  than a zero-retry success; the unchanged `0.8.20` retry later proved the fix.

## Verification

- Initial repository audit found real-data evidence for every requested method
  family, plus qualifying installed-worker evidence for several fixed and
  editable routes.
- The strongest remaining employability gaps are a fresh end-to-end
  Factor-to-Portfolio handoff and a fresh worker-owned governed-RL decision;
  multi-interval worker evidence needs conservative historical audit before it
  can count.
- Original installed CLIs independently revalidated the `0.8.19`, `0.8.22`,
  `0.8.24`, and `0.8.26` Projects and strict Explorers/Studio with zero
  diagnostics. Event staging hashes remained exactly

  and `7dad9b4eaabe1c4ce364c3ad4e6d2faef00c682d1e6728e59da4c4b8413688e2`.
